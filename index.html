Actionable guidance

Explore simple alerts and personalized hydration or rest suggestions for a wider group of athletes. The proposed sensing and alerts still require validation.

Transparent ongoing costs

Nix uses disposable patches with replacement prices not verified. A reusable sensor or affordable bundle could be explored if technically feasible.

Reliable, validated cramp-risk alerts and guidance could differentiate the concept. Neither prediction accuracy nor prevention is established.

How to read this dashboard

Representative statistics use the two identified USD upfront prices only. Mean and median use unrounded values; displayed amounts are rounded to cents. The full offer table includes all three records, including the separate GBP observation.

No exchange rate, add-on charges, taxes, shipping or duties are included. Availability, promotions and regular prices are not established in the supplied research and are labeled accordingly.

There is one researched offer per competitor; price ranges and market distributions should not be interpreted as comprehensive. Products, features and units differ. Verified refers to the recorded research status, not a live recheck.
