How the financial model works

Contribution per billed unit = price − variable cost per billed unit. Operating profit = annual revenue − annual variable costs − annual fixed operating costs. Fixed monthly amounts × 12; weekly amounts × 52. Revenue percentage costs scale with selling price.

Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ weighted contribution per billed unit, rounded up. Revenue break-even uses the unrounded contribution ratio. Sales mix is the current product unit-volume mix. Units for mixed billing models are equivalent billed units, not distinct customers. Nonpositive contribution or zero assumed volume makes mix break-even unavailable.

Startup outlays are separate cash requirements. Capital purchases may be depreciated straight-line with no residual value, no tax effects and a full year in service. Initial inventory, receivables and cash timing need separate cash-flow planning. Shared variable entries apply to each billed unit, while shared fixed overhead is counted once.
